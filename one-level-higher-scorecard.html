The One Level Higher Scorecard

Twelve real situations from Indian GCCs, IT services and operations floors. In each one, a capable senior person did something reasonable, and it quietly cost them. Answer like the room is watching.

Did you know? In a 1990 Stanford study, people tapping the rhythm of a well-known song predicted that 50% of listeners would recognise it. The actual number was 2.5%. The tapper hears the whole song. The listener hears knocking. Your work is the song. What leaves your desk is the knocking, and every situation below turns on that gap.
12 situations 3 difficulty stages 0 right-sounding jargon answers Your skill-by-skill map arrives by email

Three ground rules, because this is a diagnostic and not a quiz:

1. Answer with what you would actually do on a Tuesday, not what a book would advise.

2. More than one option is defensible in every question. You are choosing what a senior room rewards, which is a narrower thing than what is correct.

3. The last four are genuinely hard. That is deliberate. They separate operating at your level from operating one level higher.
