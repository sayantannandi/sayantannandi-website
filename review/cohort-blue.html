Before you join

A few practical details.

How is this different from books and workshops?

You’ve read the books. Maybe your company sent you to a two-day leadership workshop. It made sense in the room. By Monday, the old habits were back.

These skills change when you practice on your own work, get honest feedback, and try again the next week.

Every Saturday you bring something real: an update, a decision, a disagreement. A small group works on it with you. And you keep showing up, because people expect you.

How does the guarantee work?

In week 1, ask your manager, or another senior leader who sees your work, to rate you from 1 to 10 on three things. Ninety days after the Workshop Cohort ends, ask them again. If the average hasn't gone up, I'll refund your full fee. The three ratings are: Brings decisions, not just updates; Works across teams without friction; Ready for wider scope. To qualify, attend all 6 sessions, live or recorded, send in 5 of 6 weekly exercises, and complete both ratings. Claim by 14 May 2027. This covers how your work is seen. It doesn't cover promotions, pay or role changes. To claim, email hello@sayantannandi.com with both ratings and your purchase reference. Apart from this guarantee, fees are non-refundable. The first module is free after you log in on TagMango.

What do I keep after the Workshop Cohort?

Keep every download. Recordings available for 90 days. Download your workbooks, study guides and practice materials to keep using them after the Workshop Cohort. Alumni connections can continue beyond the program; no permanent hosted access is promised.

How does the private coaching session work?

One 60-minute one-to-one session with Sayantan, to book any time before 13 May 2027. Bring your own leadership challenge.

What are the session times in my time zone?

Six Saturdays, 9 January to 13 February 2027. Each session is 11 AM to 12:30 PM IST, 9:30 to 11 AM in Dubai, and 1:30 to 3 PM in Singapore. All sessions are on Google Meet.

Can I get a company invoice?

Yes. The provider can invoice the company directly. Email hello@sayantannandi.com with your company billing details before paying.

What if I miss a session?

Watch the recording and send in the weekly exercise. For the guarantee, attend all six sessions live or recorded and submit at least five weekly exercises.

How do recordings and confidentiality work?

Sessions are recorded for registered members. Bring anonymized or fictional examples and omit confidential employer, customer and personal information. Do not share recordings or another participant’s work outside the group. Confidentiality by other participants cannot be absolutely guaranteed.

I’m not in tech. Is this for me?

The examples often draw on technology and operations. The practice applies wherever you lead substantial work, make decisions and influence teams. Book a free call if you want to check the fit.

How is the Workshop Cohort different from the course?

The self-paced course covers the twelve lessons. The Workshop Cohort adds live sessions, a peer group, personal coaching feedback on every weekly submission, and one private coaching session. Existing course buyers should ask about their access before paying again.

What happens after I pay?

Keep your Razorpay receipt and complete the Workshop Cohort intake. Within two working days of intake and payment verification, Sayantan will email the Google Meet joining details and access instructions for lessons and workbooks.
