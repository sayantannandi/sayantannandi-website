Part one of the Time Equation

Earning Power:
make your time valuable.

What the market will pay you for who you are today.

Time is the one asset you start with. Earning Power decides what each hour of it is worth. Every rung you climb on the ladder raises it.

  1. Move from doing to deciding. Spend less time on how the work gets done, more on which work matters.
  2. Make your thinking visible. Speak in business outcomes, not activity.
  3. Let AI take the execution. It makes routine work cheaper. Judgment, context and trust stay scarce.

Part two of the Time Equation

Exit Power:
make your time yours.

How long you could say no to income and still be yourself, and how many acceptable options you would have.

High pay with a big EMI is a quiet trap. You can’t say no to the wrong project, push back in the meeting that matters, or take the better role at lower pay. Low Exit Power holds your Earning Power back. I call this the Trapped Earner.

3 months

of runway. You agree too fast and accept the first offer.

3 years

of runway. You look at the downside calmly, then decide.

  1. Count runway in months, not rupees. It is the number that changes how you behave at work.
  2. Don’t tie everything to one employer. Salary, RSUs, network and skills spread wider.
  3. Keep fixed costs below your income, not at it. Every EMI is a vote for staying.
