01Explain the value of your work
Outcome Translation · Speak Business
Outcome Translation

Turn a delivery update into an account of what changed for a customer, colleague or business process. Separate a measured result from an assumption so your claim can be checked.

Your worksheet: Changed For Log.
Speak Business

Explain your technical work through the costs, revenue, capacity or risks relevant to the decision. Keep the technical reasoning, and make its business consequences understandable.

Your worksheet: Translation Card.
02Make decisions and create room
Recommend Under Ambiguity · Deliberate Elimination
Recommend Under Ambiguity

Move a decision forward when some facts are missing. Compare options, make a recommendation and show the assumptions or new evidence that would change your view.

Your worksheet: One Page Decision.
Deliberate Elimination

Say no or renegotiate scope by explaining the consequence of adding work. Recommend what should stop, move or receive less attention, and make the choice visible to the person setting priorities.

Your worksheet: Not Doing List.
03Make your work easier to understand
Legibility · Reduce Your Boss’s Uncertainty
Legibility

Write for a leader who cannot see your daily work. Put the result, consequence or decision first, then give enough supporting detail for them to assess it.

Your worksheet: Top Line File.
Reduce Your Boss’s Uncertainty

Anticipate what your manager needs to know about progress, risk and decisions. Agree when to escalate so they can rely on your ownership without repeatedly asking for updates.

Your worksheet: Worry Ledger.
04Work across teams
Lateral Influence · Disagree Without Damage
Lateral Influence

Understand the priorities of people you do not manage, including colleagues in other functions or regions. Build a proposal that addresses their constraints and makes the exchange clear.

Your worksheet: Standing Trade.
Disagree Without Damage

State a disagreement using evidence, consequences and an alternative. Practice a respectful challenge while recognizing that the other person may still disagree with you.

Your worksheet: Disagreement Log.
05Delegate with clear responsibility
Delegate to People · Delegate to AI
Delegate to People

Transfer responsibility with clear outcomes, decision boundaries and review points. Practice giving someone room to own the work without leaving them unsupported.

Your worksheet: Delegation Ledger.
Delegate to AI

Choose an AI assignment you can evaluate, within workplace policy. Set the purpose, inputs and checks before delegating, and keep responsibility for deciding whether the result is usable.

Your worksheet: AI Delegation Ledger.
06Prepare your readiness conversation
Connect the Dots · The Readiness Conversation
Connect the Dots

Look for recurring causes across projects, teams or regions. Test a possible explanation against the evidence before recommending a broader change.

Your worksheet: Pattern Page.
The Readiness Conversation

Bring together evidence of decisions, influence and results through others. Explain the responsibility you are ready to take on and identify where more experience is still needed.

Your worksheet: Narrative Card and Readiness Note.

How to request a refund

Before you start

A few practical details.

How does the 100% refund guarantee work?

Go through Module 1. If you don’t see value, Sayantan will refund your full course fee. Email hello@sayantannandi.com from your purchase email address with your receipt or order reference to request the refund.

Must I finish in six weeks?

No. Six weeks is the curriculum structure. Study at your own pace and check the online access period shown on TagMango before purchasing.

What stays private?

The self-paced course does not require sharing company work with a group. Keep practice on your own copy and use anonymized examples if you contact Sayantan. Private messages are not shared with your employer or published without your permission. Group recording rules apply only if you separately join the Workshop Cohort.

Does the course include personal feedback or coaching?

The course includes practice scenarios and worked answers for independent review. The Workshop Cohort adds live discussion, personal coaching feedback on weekly submissions and a private coaching session.

How do I access the free first module?

Open the TagMango course link and log in. The first module is free on the platform, as configured by Sayantan.

Do I need to work in technology?

Examples mainly draw on technology, data, product and operations. The skills apply wherever you lead substantial work, make recommendations and influence teams.

What happens after I buy?

Payment and course access are handled through TagMango. Follow the platform’s purchase and login instructions. For access questions, email hello@sayantannandi.com using your purchase email address and receipt reference.

Can my company pay?

Ask hello@sayantannandi.com about an invoice in your company’s name before purchasing.

Will this get me promoted?

The course helps you practice skills and build evidence of broader responsibility. It cannot create an open role or guarantee a promotion, salary increase or employer decision.
