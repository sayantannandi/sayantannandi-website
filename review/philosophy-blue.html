Earning Power
× Exit Power
= Optionality.

First make your time valuable.
Then make it yours.

Most successful professionals earn well and still cannot afford to say no, take the better job at lower pay, or disagree in the meeting that matters.

Two powers. Real choices.

Earning Power

What the market will pay you for who you are today, given what you can do and what you can prove.

×

Exit Power

How long you could decline your income and maintain the life your household has chosen, and how many acceptable alternatives exist if that income stops.

=

Optionality

The number and quality of choices you could actually carry out, this year, without your life falling over.

The Time Equation

The first half makes your time valuable.
The second half makes it yours.

Time becomes skills. Skills become income. Some of that income becomes capital, capital becomes options, and options give you time back.

Same person.
Different room to act.

Exit Power helps you do the work that raises your Earning Power.

With three months, you may agree too quickly, avoid the project that could fail in public, or accept an offer without negotiating.

With five years, you can look at the downside calmly before deciding. Money will not give you judgment or courage. It removes one of the reasons you were not using them.

Climb, build, or slow down.

The model does not need you to want what I want.
