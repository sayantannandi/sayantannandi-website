Come as you are.
Think about three things.

No presentation needed. A few minutes with these questions makes the 20 minutes much more useful.

01

What happened.

Your last review or promotion conversation. What were you told, in their exact words?

02

What you’ve tried.

Courses, feedback, working harder, a job search. What happened each time?

03

Your runway, in months.

If your salary stopped, roughly how many months could your household manage? A rough guess is fine.

This is a 20-minute diagnosis, not a full coaching session. For an existing purchase or access question, contact support.
