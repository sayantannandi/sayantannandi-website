Before we talk

A few useful details.

Is this right for me?

It is designed for experienced managers and professionals working toward broader leadership responsibility. The first call helps us decide whether your situation is a good match.

How is this different from the course or Live Classroom?

The course gives you a structured curriculum. The Live Classroom adds group practice and feedback. Personal coaching starts with your specific situation and adapts the work around it.

What will it cost?

₹49,999 for six 60-minute sessions. We agree the final scope after the free 20-minute call, before you pay anything.

What stays private, and are sessions recorded?

Your intake, private messages and one-to-one coaching stay private. I do not contact your employer or publish your story without your permission. Private sessions are not recorded unless we both agree. Use anonymized examples. The included Live Classroom is a separate group setting: its recordings are available to registered members for 90 days, and other participants’ confidentiality cannot be absolutely guaranteed.

When do the six sessions happen, and what support is included?

Before you pay, we agree the session dates and completion window in writing, along with rescheduling arrangements, any between-session draft reviews, the contact channel and response time. Bring the conversations we select to your sessions. Additional work beyond that plan needs a separate agreement. This is scheduled coaching rather than unlimited on-call support.

What if I start before the next Live Classroom?

We confirm the included Live Classroom and its dates before payment. Private sessions can begin on the schedule we agree while you wait for the group. The package includes six private sessions in total, including the private session that comes with the Live Classroom.

What is the refund policy?

Full refund if requested before the second live session. This applies to the coaching package that includes the Live Classroom. Email hello@sayantannandi.com from your purchase address with your payment reference before the second live classroom session begins. For January 2027, that is before 11 AM IST on 16 January. After that point, fees are non-refundable. Read the refund policy.

Will coaching get me promoted?

We can work on how you make decisions, communicate your contribution and handle greater responsibility. Promotion, pay and role availability remain employer decisions.
