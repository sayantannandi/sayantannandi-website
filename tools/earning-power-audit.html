The Earning Power Audit

Earning power is what the market will pay you for who you are right now. It moves up a ladder: expertise, execution, management, strategy, influence, leadership. The rules change somewhere in the middle and nobody announces it.

Six questions. Written for people who have been told to be more strategic and were not told what that actually means.

When you describe your work to someone senior, what are you mostly describing?

In a review with your leadership, what do you typically bring?

Could you explain, in business terms, what your current work is worth to the company?

Outside your own function, how many senior people would take your call and act on your view?

The last time you disagreed with a senior stakeholder, what happened?

How much of what you are paid for could a capable person with good AI tools now do noticeably faster?

You are operating at or above your title. The constraint on you now is usually visibility and sponsorship rather than capability, which is a different problem with a different fix. Strong. You have moved past delivery into framing and influence. The gap to the next level is likely in enterprise scope: being trusted with problems that are not yours. This is the classic plateau, and it is not a criticism. You are good at your job. The trouble is that being good at your job is now the thing holding you in place, because the organisation has learned to use you for what you already do well. Most of your value currently sits in expertise and execution. That is exactly the ground that is getting cheaper. Worth treating the shift upward as urgent rather than as something for next year. Start with one thing: for the next month, replace every status update you give with a recommendation. It sounds small. It is the single fastest change most people can make.
