The Invisible Month Calculator

Open your calendar to last month before you start. Memory keeps the parts you are proud of and drops the parts nobody noticed, and this only works on the real month.

Did you know? When senior professionals run this exercise honestly, most find that between a third and half of a busy month cannot complete the sentence "somebody now does something differently because of this work." That work was real, often the hardest of the month, and it was invisible to the organisation by construction. This calculator tells you your number before your appraisal does.

Step 1 · Count the work

From last month's calendar and sent items: how many pieces of work took more than two days of your life? Projects, decks, reviews, migrations, escalations, hiring rounds. Most Senior Managers and Directors land between 6 and 12.

items last month

Step 2 · Run the sentence

For each item, try to finish this out loud: "Since we shipped [the thing], [a named team or person] now does [specific thing] instead of [old thing]." A name, not "the business." A real change in their week, not your deliverable. How many items complete the sentence?

items complete it

Step 3 · Three honesty checks

For my biggest item, a named person outside my team could confirm the change without me in the room.
In the last quarter, my boss has quoted one of my numbers back in his own update or a forum I was not in.
My last written update led with a change, not with effort, activity or a topic line.
