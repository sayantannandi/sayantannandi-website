The behaviour that got you here has quietly stopped working

Somewhere around Senior Manager, the people reading you change. They stop being people who can check your work and start being people who cannot. Your boss's boss has never seen your code, your model, your migration plan or your risk register. She cannot judge the work. She can only judge what the work did to somebody she already cares about.

So the update keeps arriving, and it keeps being true, and it keeps being ignored. The technical request gets deferred a third time. The decision you raised early comes back three weeks later having consumed four hours of senior time. And at some point somebody uses the word strategic about the person who got the role instead of you, and you decide it was politics and work harder.

It was not politics. It was format.

Who this is built for

You have twelve to twenty years in technology, data, product or digital. Senior Manager, Associate Director or Director, possibly VP. Your team respects you, your delivery record is clean, and you have been told, more than once, to be more strategic. Nobody has explained what that means in behaviour you can do on a Monday.

A good fit if

  • Your last two reviews praised your execution and questioned your influence.
  • Your boss's boss has never seen your work, only its effects.
  • You send problems upward and wonder why decisions take so long.
  • You suspect AI is repricing the half of your job you are best at.
  • You can pull one real situation from last month and rewrite it each week.

Not a fit if

  • You want interview preparation, a CV rewrite or a job search.
  • You are under five years into your career.
  • You want theory. Every module ends in a sentence you say at work.
  • You cannot give Saturday mornings to this for eight weeks.

How a week runs

  1. Saturday, 11 AM IST: live session, 90 minutes. We read three or four of last week's rewrites out loud, and I tell you what your skip-level would have read. Then the gap in the new module, and the price: what the move costs you in exposure, because every senior move has one.
  2. Saturday, after the session: the lesson opens. A ten-minute video and the workbook for the module. Why the workplace responds to it, five before-and-after pairs, one sentence with blanks.
  3. By Wednesday: your turn. You pull one real thing from your last month, an email, a status update, a decision you punted, and rewrite it using the move. This is the actual work. About forty-five minutes.
  4. By the following Friday: written feedback. I read every submission and reply to each one. Ten people, so this is a promise I can keep.
  5. The artifact. You leave each module with one thing you keep: a log, a page, a script, five lines. Twelve of them by the end.

Questions people ask before paying

What if I miss a live session?

Every session is recorded and posted the same day. Written feedback on your submission arrives whether or not you were live. Miss more than two and you will feel it, because the room is where the pairs land.

Is this for people in IT services, GCCs or product companies?

All three. The situations in the workbooks come from all three. The switch from being judged on work to being judged on effects happens in every one of them at roughly the same level.

Will my employer pay for it?

Often, yes. You get a GST invoice you can submit. If you want a two-line note to your manager on what the course covers, email me after you register and I will send one.

How much time does it take each week?

About three and a half hours: ninety minutes live, roughly forty-five minutes on the lesson, and an hour on your rewrite. Less if you are honest in the rewrite; the polishing is the part that takes time and it is the part that does not help.

Is there a self-paced version?

Yes, after this cohort ends. It has the videos and workbooks and none of the feedback. If you can do the work alone, wait for it. Most people who say that, cannot, and I include myself.

Can I pay in instalments or from outside India?

One payment for this cohort. Indian cards, UPI and net banking work on the checkout. If you are paying with an international card and it fails, email me and I will sort it.
