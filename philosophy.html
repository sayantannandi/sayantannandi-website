The philosophy

Good work should give you
more say over your life.

That is the connection between the career work and the writing about money.

A better role can bring more income and more interesting problems. It can also bring commitments that make it harder to leave. I am interested in both parts: becoming more useful at work and building enough independence to choose how much of your life work occupies.

Earning power × Exit power = Optionality.

This is a way to organise the idea, not a financial formula.

Earning power

The ability to make a useful contribution that other people can recognise and rely on. The Step Up to Senior Leadership focuses on a particular transition: explaining results and making decisions as your responsibility grows.

The six-week course and free workplace tests belong to this part of the work.

Exit power

The ability to make a choice without depending entirely on the next salary. It can mean having room to change roles or reduce work for a while. It can also mean staying in a job because you choose it.

Read about exit power →

Optionality

A growing set of realistic choices, with a clearer view of what each would cost. A higher income can help, but commitments can grow with it. The point is to consider what your work and money arrangements make possible in the life you actually want.

The essays and The Optional Career explore that connection. Contact: hello@sayantannandi.com.
