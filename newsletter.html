The weekly letter

The Optional Career

The rules of a senior career change without telling you. One letter a week on what changed and what to do about it. About a thousand words, one real situation, one number, and often something I got wrong.

No spam, and one click to leave. I do not share the list.

You are in. The first email arrives in a minute. If it lands in Promotions, drag it to Primary.

What turns up

One idea per letter. Most weeks it is about Earning Power: what makes your time worth more once capability stops being scarce. Framing a decision so somebody senior can take it in two minutes. Speaking in outcomes rather than activity. Managing up by removing worry rather than adding it. Disagreeing without starting a status fight. Saying what you will not do, and what that costs. Every third week or so, a real decision run through both halves of the arithmetic, career and money, with the working shown.

What does not

No stock tips, no market calls, no return screenshots, no urgency, and nothing that requires you to quit your job to find it useful.

Reply to it

The best thing about the letter is the replies. If something lands, or if you are stuck on a decision with a career half and a money half, write back. I read all of them, and a lot of what I write starts there.

First make your time valuable. Then make it yours.

Careers, money, and how they combine into choices. Written for senior professionals in India who are doing well on paper and want more say over what comes next.
