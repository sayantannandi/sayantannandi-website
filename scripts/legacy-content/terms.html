The terms, in plain English.

This page covers what you are buying, what you can do with it, and what I am and am not responsible for. Read alongside the privacy page and the refund rules.

What you get

The kits are digital files for your own use. The self-paced course is access to twelve modules on TagMango for as long as the course exists there, which I intend to be years, not months. The cohort is eight live sessions on the dates shown on its page, plus the course materials and recordings of the sessions.

What you can do with it
