Turn experience into
clear evidence.

A strong delivery record can be hard to explain. The course helps you show what changed because of your work and make your reasoning visible to the people deciding your next responsibility.

Make a recommendation people can use.

Connect your work to a concrete result. Explain the choice you recommend, then name the uncertainty and what you would stop to make room.

Take responsibility through others.

Work across teams and handle disagreement. Set clear boundaries when delegating to a person or to AI, and review the result before relying on it.
