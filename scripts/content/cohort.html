between sessions.

The cohort works best when you can make time to attempt the exercises and revise a piece of your own work.

Prepare an attempt.

Read the relevant material and apply it to a real situation. Identify what you know and where your reasoning still depends on an assumption.

Discuss the choices.

Use the live session to examine recommendations and consider another team’s constraints.

Submit your application.

Bring the week’s work into a focused submission. Feedback considers the reasoning and the evidence behind your proposed action.

Revise and review.

Use the response to revise your attempt. If you apply it at work, bring what happened into the next discussion.

Feedback on
the work you bring.

The focus is your application to a bounded decision or situation from your role.

A focused submission

Prepare approximately one page or its equivalent. Use the feedback and reference examples to revise your reasoning independently.

Feedback focuses on the criteria relevant to the exercise, such as the evidence supporting a recommendation or the authority in a delegation.

The support included

The cohort adds live discussion and review of your application. It does not include unlimited document editing, ongoing private coaching or an additional individual review of every revision.

If flexible study and worked answers are enough for your needs, the independent course covers the same curriculum.

Do I need to buy the independent course first?

The cohort includes the course curriculum and materials. Use the cohort checkout to review what is included before booking. If you already own the independent course, contact hello@sayantannandi.com about your existing purchase before paying again.

Does the Skills Check determine whether I can join?

No. The free check is a practice resource. Choose the cohort based on the work you want to improve and whether you can attend the sessions.

What happens after payment?

Keep your Razorpay receipt and follow the registered customer instructions. They include the intake form and explain how you receive calendar invitations and course access.

Where can I ask about fit or an existing booking?

Contact hello@sayantannandi.com. For existing purchases, your confirmed access and support commitments remain in place.
