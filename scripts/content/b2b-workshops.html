A few practical questions

Before we plan the room.

Who is this workshop designed for?

Managers and experienced professionals who lead teams, functions or major pieces of work and are preparing for broader responsibility. We agree the participant mix and seniority with you, then tailor the workshop to your organization.

What do we need to arrange?

A room with tables of five or six, a screen, printed handouts and writing materials. We’ll agree the opening speakers and manager briefing in advance. The AI exercise uses an approved tool and suitable practice material.

Can we include our own leadership situations?

Yes. A short pre-survey helps identify relevant situations. Participants bring their own work to personal exercises and choose what to share. We agree confidentiality expectations before the workshop.

How do we decide the fee and follow-up?

The proposal sets out the format, group size, location, customization, materials and follow-up, along with facilitation fees and any travel or venue requirements.
