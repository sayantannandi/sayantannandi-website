Week 1

Explain the value of your work

Lessons 1 and 2

LESSON 01

Outcome Translation

Explain what changed because of your work. Separate a measured result from an assumption.

LESSON 02

Speak Business

Connect your work to the costs, revenue or risks the person making the decision cares about.

Your worksheet: Translation Card.

Week 2

Make decisions and create room

Lessons 3 and 4

LESSON 03

Recommend Under Ambiguity

Make a recommendation when some facts are still missing. Show your assumptions and what would change your decision.

Your worksheet: One Page Decision.

LESSON 04

Deliberate Elimination

Make room for a priority by naming what will stop or receive less attention.

Your worksheet: Not Doing List.

Week 3

Make your work easier to understand

Lessons 5 and 6

LESSON 05

Legibility

Make your contribution easy to understand. Put the main point before the supporting detail.

Your worksheet: Top Line File.

LESSON 06

Reduce Your Boss’s Uncertainty

Give your manager enough information to know what needs attention and what is under control.

Your worksheet: Worry Ledger.

Week 4

Work across teams

Lessons 7 and 8

LESSON 07

Lateral Influence

Work with people you do not manage. Build a proposal that respects another team’s constraints.

Your worksheet: Standing Trade.

LESSON 08

Disagree Without Damage

Challenge a decision without turning the conversation into a personal contest.

Your worksheet: Disagreement Log.

Week 5

Delegate with clear responsibility

Lessons 9 and 10

LESSON 09

Delegate to People

Make ownership clear, including the decisions someone can make and when you will review the work.

Your worksheet: Delegation Ledger.

LESSON 10

Delegate to AI

Choose work you can hand to AI and define how you will check the output before using it.

Your worksheet: AI Delegation Ledger.

Prepare your readiness conversation

Lessons 11 and 12

LESSON 11

Connect the Dots

Look across your work for patterns that explain how you handle broader responsibility.

Your worksheet: Pattern Page.

LESSON 12

The Readiness Conversation

Build a clear account of your contribution and prepare a conversation about your next role.

Your worksheet: Narrative Card and Readiness Note.

Course details.

Do I need a management title?

The course is written for experienced managers and professionals preparing for broader responsibility. The exercises work best when you can bring a real situation from your role.

Must I finish in six weeks?

Six weeks is the curriculum sequence, with two lessons per week. The independent course lets you study at your own pace. Check the access period stated at checkout before purchase.

Does the independent course include personal feedback?

The independent course includes worked answers for your own review. The live cohort adds discussion and feedback on your workplace application.

Is this only for technology roles?

No. The practice uses different work settings. You can apply the worksheets to decisions in your own function.
