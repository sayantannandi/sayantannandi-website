Prepare an attempt
we can examine.

The cohort follows the same twelve lessons as the independent course. Live discussion and feedback help you examine how you apply them.

Before the first session

Choose one recent assignment and write a short account of what changed because of the work. Week 1 covers Outcome Translation and Speak Business. Try explaining the assignment to someone who cannot see your daily work.

Remove confidential details, or use a fictional course case. Bring a specific question about your attempt.

During the six weeks

Prepare the relevant lessons before each discussion. Follow the submission instructions for that week and keep your work focused, generally to about one page.

Feedback covers the defined submissions. Use it to revise independently; the cohort does not include unlimited editing, repeated revision reviews or open-ended individual coaching.

Sessions last 90 minutes. Your course information will specify the preparation and submission arrangements. A purchase does not automatically subscribe you to The Optional Career.
