The philosophy

First make your time valuable.
Then make it yours.

Build leadership skills to strengthen your earning power.
Use that earning power to create more choice.

Earning power grows with the contribution you can make.

My work is for experienced professionals who want to increase their earning power by growing into leadership. A senior role asks you to take responsibility for more than your own output. You need to make decisions that other people can act on and create the conditions for them to deliver. That is a different contribution, and it requires a different set of skills.

More years of experience do not automatically make that transition happen.

Learn the skills. Replace the habits.

The habits that made you reliable can become a limit. Taking every task yourself leaves other people waiting for you. Saying yes to every request hides the cost of a new priority. Reporting everything you did can make it harder to see the decision you recommend.

Upskilling means practising a different response. Learn to explain an outcome and make a recommendation under uncertainty. Develop the ability to delegate with clear boundaries. These skills help you demonstrate the broader contribution a leadership role requires.

A practical route into that work.

The free tests help you identify a practice focus. They examine your choices in workplace situations and explain the reasoning behind a stronger response.

The Step Up to Senior Leadership course provides twelve lessons across six weeks, with study guides and practice books. The live cohort adds discussion and feedback as you apply the same skills to your own work.

The aim is to build the contribution and evidence behind a senior role. A promotion or pay rise still depends on the role available and how your employer evaluates that evidence.

Earning power is the first part. Exit power is the next.

A higher income can give you room to build financial flexibility. What you do with that room affects the choices you eventually have. Exit power is the ability to make a work or life decision without depending entirely on the next salary.

Leadership is the career path this course develops. It is not the only way to earn well. The wider purpose is to make your skills more valuable and turn that value into more say over your life.

Earning power × Exit power = Optionality.

The equation brings the two ideas together. It does not calculate a financial outcome.
