Exit power

The room to make a choice
about your work.

A higher income and more freedom do not always arrive together.

A role can pay well and still feel difficult to leave. Regular commitments may need most of the income. Family responsibilities may narrow the alternatives. The next move may take time to build. Exit power is my name for the room you have to make a choice while recognising those constraints.

Leaving is only one possible use of that room.

The money side

The useful starting point is a clear view of your recurring costs and the money you can actually access. Debt payments and money already committed to a future need affect that picture. A single wealth number does not describe all of it.

The practical side

A change in work also depends on family arrangements and the alternatives available to you. A long break and a move to another employer involve different decisions, even for someone with the same savings.

The connection to earning power

Skills that remain useful across roles can widen your options. The course addresses the work side of that equation, especially how you make recommendations and take broader responsibility. It does not provide an investment plan or decide when someone can stop working.
