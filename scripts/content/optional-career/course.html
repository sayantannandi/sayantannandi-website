Who it is for

Managers who are good at their job, and who see themselves in some of these.

  • You were told to "be more strategic", and nobody said what to do differently.
  • Your updates are full of work, and your director still cannot repeat them.
  • You were rated "exceeds" again, and the promotion went to someone else.
  • You got a raise, and the EMI grew with it.
  • Last year's bonus went on a holiday and a phone.
  • You disagree in your head and agree in the meeting.

The path, in eight steps

Steps one to four teach four skills that help you earn more, in your current company or when you move. They are open now. Steps five to eight, on depending less on your salary and on your own next decision, are being built.

StepPartWhat you work on
1Earn moreThe ceiling: what your next level is paid for, and the habit you carried up
2Earn moreShow what your work changed: in updates, in interviews and when you ask for more pay
3Earn moreDecide without all the facts
4Earn moreGet help from people who do not report to you
5Depend lessWhere the raise goes: fixed costs and the months test
6Depend lessYour runway in months, and three ways to add to it
7Depend lessHow much rides on one employer, and who you could call
8ChooseYour own next decision, worked through: climb, build or slow down

The money steps are education: arithmetic and decisions about your own numbers. They are not investment advice. Nobody can promise you a promotion, and I do not.
