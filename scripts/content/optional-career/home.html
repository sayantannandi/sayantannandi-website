The first way to get stuck: the ceiling

Each level at work pays for something different. Most people keep doing what the last level paid for, because it is what got them promoted. The third step is where most get stuck.

The second way to get stuck: the trap

Every raise finds a home. A bigger flat, a car, a better holiday. Pay goes up, costs follow, and the number of months you could last without the salary stays where it was, or falls.

Raise spent

₹30,000 more a month, all of it turned into new costs. Runway goes from 8 months to about 6.7.

Raise split

Half becomes costs and half moves out on salary day. A year later, runway is about 8.4 months.

A made-up household. The working is in the essay.

Put the two together

High pay with little runway is the Trapped Earner: worth a lot, and unable to risk a no. Raise both, and you have an Optional Career. You can push for the bigger role, say no to a bad one, or slow down, and afford each.
