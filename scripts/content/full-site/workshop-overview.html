Leadership development · Program overview

STAMP

Speak, Think, Act, Manage up and Persuade like a leader.

For managers and experienced professionals preparing for wider responsibility. An in-person workshop around their own work, with research, exercises and rehearsal.

1 or 2 days
20–30 participants
In person

What participants work on

  • Speak: rewrite an update around a business outcome and a clear ask.
  • Think: explain competing priorities and recommend what moves out.
  • Act: delegate an outcome with boundaries and suitable AI checks.
  • Manage up: rehearse a difficult conversation and plan a sponsor ask.
  • Persuade: prepare a cross-team request that recognizes other priorities.

What the organization receives

A scoped agenda, participant materials and practical work produced in the room. Pre-work and 30-day or 90-day follow-up are agreed in the proposal.

Your facilitator

Sayantan Nandi. Former IT Director at Schneider Electric, with fifteen years in technology and experience leading teams of more than 200 people. ISB MBA.

Scope & next step

Agree the participant mix, priorities, dates and venue. Fees reflect preparation, facilitation, customization, materials, travel and agreed follow-up.
