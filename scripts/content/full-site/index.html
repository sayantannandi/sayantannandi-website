An example exercise: Finance has verified its month-end close is one day faster after 11 service migrations.

Before · What the team did
“This sprint the team migrated 11 services and closed 42 tickets.”

The activity is clear. The business effect is missing.

After · What the room can act on
“Finance now closes the month a day earlier. The remaining four migrations need your vendor-contract decision by Friday.”

A result, an open decision and a date.

We’d check the evidence behind the result, make the decision explicit and rehearse how you’d explain it. Then you’d use the revised update in your actual conversation.
