Earning Power

The value you can
take with you.

Your salary is one agreement with one employer. Your earning power also includes your judgment, skills and the evidence that helps someone trust you with a bigger problem.

It grows when you can make a useful call, influence beyond your authority and help others deliver.

Exit Power

The room to
consider another path.

Savings and manageable commitments can give you time to search, learn or change direction. That room matters even when you want to stay employed.

You may negotiate with less urgency, accept a role with better work or take a break you can support.

What optionality can look like

A choice you can
actually afford to make.

Imagine two people offered a lower-paying role with better work and less travel. One has room in the household budget and savings to absorb a setback. The other needs every rupee of the next salary.

They can value the same things and make different decisions. Capability opens doors. Financial room changes which doors you can walk through.

Neither power has to mean leaving work. You might keep growing, build something alongside your job or choose a slower pace. The point is to understand the exchange before the commitments make it for you.

An illustrative situation. The equation is a thinking framework, rather than a numerical model.
