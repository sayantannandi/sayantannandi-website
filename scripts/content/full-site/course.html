A lesson in practice

A better answer to “Can we do both?”

Illustrative exercise. Two requests need 20 hours each. Your team has 30 hours available.

The familiar response

“We’ll try to get both done.”

The ten-hour shortfall stays hidden until something slips.

The response to practice

“I recommend the customer fix first and the internal report next week. Keeping both dates needs ten more hours of capacity.”

A visible constraint, a recommendation and a choice.
