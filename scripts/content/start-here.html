can fill your entire week.

A broader role asks you to make room for a different kind of contribution.

In the first part of a career, being dependable and knowing the detail can earn you more responsibility. Around the first decade, some roles begin to ask for something else: a recommendation when the facts are incomplete, or a clear explanation of why the work matters. The timing varies. A title or a number of years does not decide it, but the expectations can change before anyone explains them.

Doing more of the same can leave less time to practise what the next role requires.
