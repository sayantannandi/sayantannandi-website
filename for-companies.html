Stronger middle managers, and leaders with a clear head about money

Two programs for your people. Taught online, at your office, or at a camp in the hills.

Write to me
Program one

Leaders of tomorrow

For managers who are stuck between managing the work and choosing which work matters. Four skills, taught on their own real work: showing what their work changed, deciding without all the facts, getting help across teams, and framing choices for the people above them.

Two days in a room, or six weeks online. Groups of 15 to 25.

Program two

Money clarity for leaders

For senior managers and above. Each person works out their runway in months, how much of their pay goes to costs they cannot stop, and how much of their household rides on one employer. They leave with their own numbers on one page.

A half day or one day. Education only: no products, and no advice on what to buy or sell.

Why a company would pay for the second one

A leader who is one bad quarter away from trouble at home avoids hard calls. They agree in the meeting and worry at night. People who can see their own numbers make calmer decisions, and they speak up sooner.

I cannot promise that this keeps people from leaving. What it does is remove one reason good people panic, and it turns staying into something they choose.
