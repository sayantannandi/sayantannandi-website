For the point where
the work gets broader.

You may be preparing for a senior role, new to one, or already doing the work without a change in title. What matters is having decisions and relationships on which to practise.

This course fits when you…

  • Deliver reliably and now need to recommend what should happen next.
  • Work across functions with different priorities and measures of success.
  • Need to make room for new work instead of quietly absorbing it.
  • Want people to take ownership without depending on you for every decision.

Bring a real situation.

A resource request, a delayed launch, an operational change or a decision with competing priorities is enough. Keep it small enough to examine closely and remove confidential details.

The outcome is better work you can show and improve. A promotion or salary increase is not a promised result.

What you will practise

Eight modules.
Eight useful pieces of work.

The skills build on one another. The final module brings them into an integrated leadership brief for a decision of your own.

01

Explain business outcomes

Connect the work to what changed for the business. Separate a measured result from an assumption, and capacity released from cash saved.

You build: an evidence-based outcome statement.

02

Communicate a clear point

Help a busy audience understand the decision, why it matters and what you need from them. Keep the detail available for scrutiny.

You build: a decision-first update.

03

Make a sound recommendation

Compare credible options, examine uncertainty and make a recommendation that another person can question and act on.

You build: an options and recommendation brief.

04

Set priorities and make room

Make the cost of a new priority explicit. Work within genuine constraints and agree what will stop, move or receive less attention.

You build: a priority and capacity agreement.

05

Build influence across teams

Understand another function’s responsibilities and risks. Make a proposal that addresses their constraints as well as your own.

You build: a stakeholder conversation plan.

06

Handle challenge and disagreement

Stay useful when your reasoning is questioned. Check the basis of the disagreement, update when needed and make the next step clear.

You build: a response to a difficult challenge.

07

Delegate with clear ownership

Give someone an outcome, decision authority, boundaries and a review point. Create room for judgment while retaining accountability.

You build: an ownership and delegation agreement.

08

Bring the skills into one decision

Combine evidence, options, trade-offs, stakeholder needs and ownership in a decision from your own work.

You build: your integrated leadership brief.

Enough explanation
to understand the why.

Every module explains why the skill is taught, why senior responsibility calls for it and what changes when you apply it well.

01 / UNDERSTAND

A substantial workbook

Eight modules of explanations, worked examples, reflection questions and practice. Follow the reasoning instead of memorising a phrase.

02 / COMPARE

Examples across contexts

Core before-and-after examples plus four extended scenarios per module, spanning functions and industries. Each case makes the evidence, constraint and choice visible.

03 / REVIEW

Reference answers & research

An answer book to review your attempts. Research notes explain what a study examined, what it suggests, its limits and how it relates to the practice.

A practical study rhythm: read the explanation, attempt the exercise before opening the answer, compare the reasoning, then revise a piece of your own work. Give yourself time for application as well as reading.

A few useful questions.

Do I need to work in technology?

No. The course uses situations from different functions and industries. You need a work situation in which you can practise making a recommendation, setting a priority or working through other people.

Does independent study include personal feedback?

It includes explanations and reference answers for self-review. Individual review of your workplace submissions belongs to the live cohort within its stated feedback scope.

Can I join the cohort after studying independently?

Yes, you can express interest in a later group. Any available course-buyer credit, seat availability and registration terms will be shown before you pay. Existing purchases keep their agreed terms.

Can I see a full example first?

Yes. The free sample includes the situation, an initial response, a stronger recommendation, annotations, a practice prompt and an answer. You can also download a blank leadership brief.
