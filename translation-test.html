The Translation Test

Every technical fact trades at an exchange rate when it reaches a room that spends money. This test measures yours. Eight real statements, three rewrites each. Pick the one the room can spend.

Did you know? Kahneman and Tversky found that the same fact framed as a loss moves people about twice as hard as the identical fact framed as a gain. And an undated risk is discounted to nearly zero by every reader, including you. Which means two accurate sentences about the same work can produce completely different funding decisions. Most senior professionals send the wrong one for years without knowing.
The rules. Every statement below is true, and so is every rewrite. Nothing is exaggerated. In each question, one version converts the fact into a currency somebody in the room is measured on. The other two are traps: one stays in technical or activity currency, and one crosses the line into marketing. You get the answer immediately after each pick. Nobody is watching, so pick what you would actually send.
