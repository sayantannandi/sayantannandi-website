STAMP · Speak, Think, Act, Manage up, Persuade

1 or 2 daysFormat around your priorities
20–30 peopleMixed groups of managers
5 leadership skillsApplied to their own work
In personYour office or venue

The work in the room

Five skills. Tangible outputs.

Select a skill to see what participants practice and take back to work.

Speak in business outcomes

An update a senior leader can act on.

Practice explaining what changed for the business, the evidence behind it and the decision needed next.

What they leave with

One real update rewritten, outcome first, ready to send.

The workshop flow

Time to understand.
Time to try.

Research, shared activities, peer rehearsal and application to their own work.

Day 1 · Decisions & outcomes

What the next level asks for.

  • Leadership Value Ladder and Promotion Committee exercise
  • Speak: business outcomes and the tapping activity
  • Think: priorities and the Not-Doing game
  • Act: delegation boundaries and AI checks

Day 2 · Influence & visibility

Get seen, heard and backed.

  • Visibility, sponsors and the 20-minute ask
  • Manage up: a clear pitch, saying no and disagreement
  • Persuade: influence and the ultimatum game
  • Five dated actions and two development goals

One focused day

All five skills. One activity for each.

The shorter format retains Speak, Think, Act, Manage up and Persuade, with less time for extended role-plays and sponsorship work. We agree the emphasis around your group’s needs.
