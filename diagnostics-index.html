Seven free diagnostics, two to eight minutes each

Start with the sentence you were given.

Every diagnostic here begins with something a senior professional has actually been told, or has thought at eleven at night. Pick the one that sounds like you. Each takes between two and eight minutes, each ends with a number, and each sends the reading by email with the one move that changes it. All free.

The two most people need

Nobody can see most of what I did this year.

Three numbers from last month: how many pieces of work took more than two days, how many changed what a named person does, how many have a before and after number. The gap between the first and the third is the part of your year that does not exist in the room.

The Invisible Work Calculator Two minutes. Tests Module 1, Outcome Translation.

I have been told to be more strategic.

Twelve situations from Indian GCCs, IT services and operations, one per senior behaviour. Answer like the room is watching. The reading maps all twelve and names the two holding your case back, which is usually not the two you expected.

The One Level Higher Scorecard Eight minutes. Tests all twelve modules.

Five more, one behaviour each

If the scorecard has already told you where the gap is, these go deeper on one behaviour at a time. Each is built from real situations, and in each one three of the four options are reasonable. That is what makes them useful.

  • The Calibration Room Test

    Six real update snippets. Which sentences survive the room where promotions are decided, and which get forwarded with FYI on top and then forgotten? Three minutes.

    Module 2, Legibility

  • The Translation Test

    Eight technical statements, three rewrites each. Pick the one a CFO room can actually spend. Four minutes, instant answers.

    Module 4, Reword Technical to Business

  • The Dependency Test

    Seven stuck situations where everyone is polite and nothing moves. Can you move people who do not owe you anything? Six minutes.

    Modules 6 and 7, Reduce Uncertainty and Lateral Influence

  • The Sixty-Five Percent Test

    Six senior decisions where the information is not coming. Every option is defensible. The hardest test on this site.

    Module 10, Taking Calls Under Ambiguity

  • The Readiness Score

    Twelve situations from product companies and GCCs, scored for which senior skills you already run. A different set of situations from the scorecard, for anyone who wants a second reading.

What happens after

Each reading arrives by email with a worksheet. If the behaviour it names is the one costing you, the course teaches all twelve, with sixty before-and-after pairs. If you have a promotion cycle or an annual review coming, the Promotion Kit is the faster route. And if the number is fine, you will know that too, and you can stop here with a clear conscience.

The Optional Career

One letter a week. The rules of a senior career change without telling you. Each issue takes one of those changes, one real situation and one number, and works out what to do. About a thousand words. Read the latest issue and the promise before you join, if you like.
