The Dependency Test

Roughly seventy percent of what you now need comes from people who do not report to you and are measured on something completely different. This test measures whether you can move them.

Did you know? A 2016 study in Nature Communications found that not knowing whether something bad will happen is more stressful than knowing it will. Stress peaked when the odds were fifty-fifty and dropped when the bad outcome became certain. The person sitting on your approval, your dependency, your unanswered case is usually not short of time. He is short of certainty. That is the currency this test is about, and it is the one currency you own outright.
The rules. Seven situations, all real shapes from Indian workplaces: GCCs, IT services, banks, FMCG, e-commerce. In each one, something is stuck and everyone involved is being perfectly polite. Every option is professional and defensible. One of them moves the file. You will not be told which until the end, so do not read backwards from what sounds clever. Pick what you would actually do on a Tuesday.
