The Step Up to Senior Leadership:
Live Cohort

Practice the skills for senior and global leadership.
Get feedback on how you apply them.

For managers in GCCs, IT services and multinational companies who want responsibility for larger portfolios and work across teams or countries. The cohort pairs twelve lessons with six live sessions to develop business judgment, communication, influence and delegation.

Bring the situations where your familiar response no longer helps: a request you cannot absorb, a disagreement you keep avoiding or work that still waits for your approval. Use the course to prepare a response, then examine your reasoning through discussion and feedback.

Six weeks to practice
a different way of working.

Choose the cohort if you want a fixed weekly commitment and feedback on your application. You might bring a recommendation for a global stakeholder, a capacity trade-off or a responsibility you need to delegate. Use the material
between sessions.

You can understand an idea and still return to an old habit under pressure. The weekly rhythm gives you time to prepare a response, examine it and try again.

Prepare an attempt.

Read the relevant material and apply it to a real situation. Identify what you know and where your reasoning still depends on an assumption.

Discuss the choices.

Examine why a recommendation might work for your team but create a problem for another function or region. Practice explaining the trade-off and considering an alternative.

Submit your application.

Bring the week’s work into a focused submission. Feedback considers the reasoning and the evidence behind your proposed action.

Revise and review.

Use the response to revise your attempt. If you apply it at work, bring what happened into the next discussion.

Feedback on
the work you bring.

For example, a recommendation can sound confident while leaving the capacity problem unresolved. Feedback examines whether the evidence supports your proposal, who carries the consequences and what the other person is being asked to decide.

A focused submission

Prepare approximately one page or its equivalent. Use the feedback and reference examples to revise your reasoning independently.

Feedback focuses on the criteria relevant to the exercise, such as the evidence supporting a recommendation or the authority in a delegation.

The support included

The cohort adds live discussion and review of your application. It does not include unlimited document editing, ongoing private coaching or an additional individual review of every revision.

If flexible study and worked answers are enough for your needs, the independent course covers the same curriculum.

The cohort includes the course curriculum and materials. Use the cohort checkout to review what is included before booking. If you already own the independent course, contact hello@sayantannandi.com about your existing purchase before paying again.

Will this secure a promotion or global role?

The cohort gives you structured practice and feedback on the skills behind broader responsibility. Appointments also depend on available roles, your experience and the organization’s decisions. A promotion, overseas move or pay increase is not guaranteed.

No. The free check is a practice resource. Choose the cohort based on the work you want to improve and whether you can attend the sessions.
