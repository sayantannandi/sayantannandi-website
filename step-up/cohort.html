The live session is
one part of the practice.

The work between sessions matters. Choose this format when you can protect time for preparation, submission and revision.

BEFORE / PREPARE

Attempt the work.

Read the module and make a first attempt using a real situation. Notice where the evidence, decision rights or constraints are still unclear.

TOGETHER / EXAMINE

Test your reasoning.

Discuss realistic situations, consider another function’s perspective and work through the choices that change a recommendation.

AFTER / APPLY

Revise and use it.

Submit the week’s work, review the feedback and decide what to try. Bring the effect of that attempt into the next discussion.

Feedback with
a clear focus.

The planned format is a review of your application to a bounded decision. The final schedule and response arrangements will be supplied before registration.

Planned submission scope

  • One submission per module, approximately one page or its equivalent.
  • One consolidated response against that module’s criteria.
  • Feedback on reasoning, evidence, trade-offs, clarity and ownership as relevant.
  • Independent revision using the comments and reference examples.

Choose the support you will use.

The cohort is suited to someone who wants discussion and a review of their attempt. It does not include unlimited document editing, ongoing private coaching or an additional individual review of every revision.
