You were trusted to deliver.
The next role asks for more.

  • You send a clean status update. Your VP replies, “What do you need from me?”
  • You’ve heard “be more strategic” in two reviews, without an example of what to change.
  • A new priority lands. You say yes, then work late to fit it in.
  • Two teams disagree, and the decision lands on your boss’s desk again.

These situations ask for judgment, influence and ownership that strong delivery alone may not demonstrate.

Why practice needs a place in your week.

A leadership workshop can make sense in the room while leaving Monday’s habits untouched.

In this cohort you bring an anonymized update, decision or disagreement, work through it, and use feedback to revise your attempt.

Every Saturday gives you a reason to prepare something concrete. The following week gives you a chance to try again.
