Work through eight situations that ask you to move beyond task delivery. Compare your choices with the leadership reasoning in each explanation. This additional practice stays on the page without an email signup.

Practise a different response.

These are fictional situations with limited information. Choose the strongest response given the facts provided. You can change an answer before opening its explanation.

At the end, choose one skill to practise in your own work. This check does not measure your overall leadership ability or predict a promotion.
