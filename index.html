One decision, three lenses, one number

A Director, 42, on ₹1 crore of cash. An offer at ₹70 lakh to own a whole function and report to the CEO. The role is the one he says he wants. Earning power says go. Then the half of the analysis nobody runs.

₹35 lakh liquid₹3.4 lakh a month
=
10months of runway

On the new salary he adds about ₹60,000 a month instead of ₹2.1 lakh, so a new month of runway takes six months to buy instead of seven weeks. He is not losing runway. He is losing the ability to build it, at 42, with two children heading into their expensive years. The verdict is not yet, and not yet is a date. Give the same man ₹1.4 crore liquid instead of ₹35 lakh and the answer reverses completely.
