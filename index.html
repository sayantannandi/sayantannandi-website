Growing into leadership is a way to increase the value of your contribution and strengthen your earning power. But the habits that made you a reliable individual contributor can become a limit: taking on every task yourself or reporting progress without making a recommendation.

The Step Up to Senior Leadership helps you build the skills for that transition. Start with a free test to find a practice focus, then use the course or live cohort to develop it.

Your next role needs
more than more effort.

If your next stage of earning power depends on a senior role, your development needs to match that role. Learn to make decisions and get results through other people, while showing why those results matter.

Replace reporting with a recommendation.

An activity update says what happened. A useful recommendation helps someone decide what should happen next. Practise that shift with evidence from your own work.

Replace doing everything with clear ownership.

Taking on every task can make you indispensable to delivery and leave little room for leadership. Learn to delegate with clear authority and review the result without taking the work back.
