The central idea

Build a career that creates choices, not just a larger salary.

Most careers are designed to improve one number: what you earn. I write about the second number too: how much control you have over your time, and how deliberately you can choose what happens next.

The work sits at the intersection of careers, money, decision-making and optionality.
