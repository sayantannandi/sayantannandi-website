Start with a free workplace check, find a skill to practise and explore what greater responsibility could look like in your work.

Early in a career, you can build a reputation by knowing the detail and delivering reliably. As you move towards leadership, your value increasingly comes from the decisions you make and the results you enable through others. That transition calls for new skills. Working longer hours or taking on every difficult task can leave you tied to the work you already know, with little time to practise the work of the next role.

Upskilling also means letting go of habits that have stopped helping.

Two checks.
A starting point for upskilling.

The first check looks at your judgment. The second looks at how you explain your contribution. Both point to leadership skills you can practise through the course or cohort.
