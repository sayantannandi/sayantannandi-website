The Sixty-Five Percent Test

Around Director, the questions stop having answers. The information does not exist, cannot arrive in time, or costs more than the decision is worth. This test measures what you do with the gap.

Did you know? In a study run by Stuart Oskamp, professionals were given case information in stages and asked for a judgement plus a confidence rating at each stage. As information accumulated, confidence climbed steadily. Accuracy rose early and then stopped. The gap between those two lines is where most senior careers quietly stall: waiting for one more data point does not feel like waiting. It feels like being careful. Meanwhile the option you were evaluating closes, and a closed option has no name in any review.
Fair warning. This is the hardest test on this site, on purpose. Six situations, all real shapes. Every option is defensible; several are what genuinely capable people do. In each case, one option is what a senior room ultimately rewards, and the margin between it and the second-best option is exactly where careers separate. Your score appears at the end.
