The terms, in plain English.

For an existing purchase, the price and terms agreed when you paid continue to apply. This update does not reduce those commitments.

Who you are dealing with

This site and everything sold on it is run by Sayantan Nandi, an individual based in India. Payments for the kits and the cohort are processed by Razorpay, and for the self-paced course by TagMango. Prices are in Indian rupees. The January 2027 cohort is ₹17,999 including 18% GST, as stated on its checkout. Review the relevant checkout and receipt for other purchases.

What you get

The kits are digital files for your own use. The Step Up to Senior Leadership independent course provides twelve lessons across six weeks on TagMango for as long as the course exists there, which I intend to be years, not months. The Step Up to Senior Leadership live cohort includes six live sessions across six weeks on the dates shown on its page, plus the course materials and recordings of the sessions.

What you can do with it

Use it in your own career, share it with your own team if you lead one, and quote from it with a mention of where it came from. What you cannot do is resell it, upload it to a shared drive for a company or a community, or reproduce it in your own paid product. The workbooks carry a licence line for a reason.

It is education, drawn from my own experience and reading. It is not a promise of a promotion, a raise or any outcome, and it is not personalised advice. Nothing on this site is financial, legal or tax advice, and I am not a SEBI-registered investment adviser. Decisions you make after reading or attending are yours.

The January 2027 cohort

Six Saturday sessions run from 9 January to 13 February, 11 AM to 12:30 PM IST on Google Meet, for up to 20 participants. Recordings are available for 90 days after the final session. Course material access continues for as long as the course is hosted. One focused submission is included each week, due Tuesday at 11:59 PM IST, with one written feedback response within three working days. The same timing applies to the final week. Late submissions are reviewed when capacity allows. Unlimited editing and ongoing private coaching are not included.

Sessions are recorded for the group. By joining you agree to be recorded, and you agree not to share recordings or other participants' situations outside the group. Use anonymized or fictional examples. Keep confidential employer, customer and personal information out of discussions and submissions. Participants must respect these rules; absolute confidentiality about peer behavior cannot be guaranteed. I may use a situation from a session in later writing, but only unrecognisably and only after asking.
