The Invisible Work Calculator

How much of your working month can your organisation actually see? Most senior professionals have never run this number on themselves. It takes two minutes, and it stings.

Did you know? In annual calibration, most companies give each person six to eight minutes of discussion, argued by a manager working entirely from memory. Whatever he cannot repeat about your year does not exist in that room. This calculator measures how much of your month survives that filter.

You will need one thing open in another tab: your calendar for the last four weeks. Not your memory. Memory keeps the parts you are proud of and drops the parts nobody noticed.

Three numbers · Be honest, nobody is watching

Your last four weeks

1 · The work

Scanning your calendar and sent items: how many pieces of work took more than two days of your life in the last four weeks? Projects, decks, reviews, migrations, escalations, hiring rounds. Count them all.

8

Most Senior Managers and Directors land between 6 and 15.

2 · The visible work

For how many of those can you complete this sentence, honestly, right now: \u201CBecause of this, [a named person or team] now does [a specific thing] differently.\u201D A name, not \u201Cthe business.\u201D A changed Tuesday, not an output delivered.

4

If you are stretching to make the sentence fit, it does not count. That stretch is the entire finding.

3 · The defensible work

Of those, how many have a before number and an after number you could say out loud in a review without checking? \u201C2pm to 7am.\u201D \u201CSix days to 22 hours.\u201D \u201CNine Sev-1s to three.\u201D

2

This is usually the smallest number, and it is the only one a calibration room can use.
