What runway changes in a meeting

Two managers, the same pay and the same bad plan. One of them speaks.

Picture two managers. They are made up, and they earn the same ₹45 lakh a year. They sit in the same meeting, hearing the same plan from the same boss, and both think it will miss its date.

One has 3 months of runway. The other has 14.

The first one nods, and complains to a friend that evening. The second says, "I think this misses the date. This is what I would do instead."

The second manager is no braver than the first. A no simply costs him less. For him it is a trade he can price. For the first manager it is a risk to his home.

I know the first seat. At 33, with eight months of runway, I sat in a meeting, saw a decision going the wrong way, and said nothing.

Now look at what happens next. The manager who speaks up with a recommendation is doing what promotion reviews look for. So his runway raises his level, and his level raises his pay, which can add to his runway. The two numbers feed each other. That is why I say they multiply.

What you do with both is your call. Some people use them to keep climbing. Some build something of their own. Some slow down. Staying where you are counts too, once it is a choice.

Your turn

Think of the last meeting where you stayed quiet. At how many months of runway would you have spoken?
