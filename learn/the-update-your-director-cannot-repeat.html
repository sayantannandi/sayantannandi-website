The update your director cannot repeat

A forwarded update gets ten seconds from someone who has never met you.

This is an update a team might send after a good sprint:

"This sprint the team migrated 11 services and closed 42 tickets."

It is true, and the team worked hard for it. Now picture the director forwarding it to her own boss. All she can repeat is "they migrated some services".

The same sprint, written for the person who has to repeat it:

"Finance now closes the month a day earlier. The remaining four migrations need your vendor-contract decision by Friday."

Nothing about the work changed between the two. The second one answers three things a busy reader is asking.

  1. Who can now do what? Finance closes a day earlier.
  2. What number moved? One day, every month.
  3. What do you need from me, and by when? A decision, by Friday.

The first version makes the reader work out why it matters, and most readers will not do that work. The second can be repeated in a lift.

The numbers 11 and 42 are made up.

Your turn

Take the last update you sent. Underline every verb about your own team: migrated, closed, built. Then rewrite the first line so it starts with someone outside your team.
