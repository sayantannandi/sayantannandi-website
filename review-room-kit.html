Inside the kit.

8-page workbook + editable one-page Word template. Filled-in examples accompany the exercises so you can compare them with your own work.

SECTION 1

Claims that show an outcome

Rewrite activity statements to explain what changed because of the work.

SECTION 2

A difficult quarter

Describe a missed result honestly, using the worked example to organise the explanation.

SECTION 3

The four-rooms check

Examine how a claim reads to your manager and to people who know less about your work.

SECTION 4

The feedback decoder

Turn broad comments such as “be more strategic” into a question you can discuss.

SECTION 5

The one-pager

Prepare the editable summary your reviewer can carry into the discussion.
