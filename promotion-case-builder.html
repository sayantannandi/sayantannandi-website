The Promotion Case Builder

Included Included free for course and cohort members.

Build a clear case for the leadership responsibility you are ready to take on.

A working download for professionals building their earning power through a leadership career. Use evidence from your work to show the responsibility you can already handle. The examples are written around Indian professionals in global organisations.

Inside the kit.

10-page workbook + 3-sheet Excel. Filled-in examples accompany the exercises so you can compare them with your own work.

SECTION 1

Mine your year

Find evidence in your calendar and sent items instead of relying only on memory.

SECTION 2

The calibration sentence

Write a concise claim that someone else can repeat accurately.

SECTION 3

The 12-behaviour gap audit

Use the workbook’s evidence prompts to identify where your case needs more support.

SECTION 4

Evidence of broader responsibility

Connect your examples to work expected in the next role.

SECTION 5

The pre-ask conversation

Prepare questions for your manager before the promotion cycle opens.

SECTION 6

The one-page case

Bring the evidence into a page your manager can use in a review discussion.

A focused piece of work.

Three to four hours, usually across a week.

This is a downloadable workbook included with the course and cohort. It does not include a personal review of your work. The six-week course provides the broader curriculum; the live cohort adds discussion and feedback.
