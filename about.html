About

I spent fifteen years building earning power. Then I started thinking much harder about what it was supposed to buy.

I am Sayantan Nandi. I have spent my career in technology leadership, studied at ISB, led large teams and eventually reached the point where I could step away and ask a more useful question: what is a successful career for?

The short version

Technology leader.
ISB MBA.
Investor.
Writer.

Now exploring how careers and money can create more control over time.

I built a technology career. Expertise, then delivery, then teams, then the kind of role where people stop asking how and start asking whether. An MBA from ISB somewhere in the middle. The salary went up on a fairly reliable schedule and I did what most people in that position do, which is assume that the number going up was the whole job.

In parallel, mostly by instinct rather than by design, I was doing the other thing. Saving, investing, keeping commitments smaller than they could have been. I did not have a name for it at the time. I only understood what it had been buying me when I tried to make a decision that a lot of people at my level cannot make, and found that I could.

That gap is what I write about now. Not because I solved anything, but because I ran the audit on my own life, wrote down the arithmetic, and found that most of it applied to people I had worked alongside for years without any of us ever discussing it.

What I am doing now

I am on a sabbatical from a corporate career. I am using it to read, write, travel, learn, invest and have long conversations with professionals who are asking some version of the same question: how do I make the career I built create more choices rather than fewer?

What I believe

A career should eventually give you more control over your time, not less. Earning power matters enormously, but it is only half of the system. The other half is converting some of that income into runway, resilience and the freedom to make a decision without needing the next salary cheque to approve it.

First make your time valuable. Then make it yours.
