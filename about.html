How I approach the work

Make the situation specific.
Then examine the thinking.

“Be more strategic” is difficult feedback to use. A recommendation with missing evidence, a meeting without a clear ask or a decision that keeps returning to your boss gives us something concrete to work with.

We can examine the assumptions, prepare a different response and see what happens when you use it. That is the starting point for the coaching, lessons and workshops.

I also write about the relationship between earning power and exit power. A stronger career and more financial room can support a wider set of choices about how you live and work.
