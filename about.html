I’m Sayantan Nandi. I spent fifteen years in technology leadership and have an MBA from the Indian School of Business.

I teach and write about the work that broader leadership roles ask of us, and what a successful career should make possible.

The work behind the title.

My course, The Step Up to Senior Leadership, focuses on eight practical skills: explaining outcomes, communicating a clear point, making recommendations, setting priorities, influencing across teams, handling disagreement, delegating ownership and bringing the skills into one decision.

The aim is to help you understand the reasoning, practise it in different situations and use it on work of your own.

The larger philosophy.

Earning power × Exit power = Optionality. Build skills and a contribution others value. Alongside that, build credible choices about the work and life you want next.

The Optional Career is where I explore both sides. The course develops a practical part of earning power; the writing keeps the wider purpose in view.
