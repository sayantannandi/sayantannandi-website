About Sayantan

Leadership skills.
Earning power.
More choice.

I’m Sayantan Nandi. I spent fifteen years in technology leadership and have an MBA from the Indian School of Business.

I help experienced professionals build the skills to grow into leadership and strengthen their earning power. My writing connects that career development with more choice over life.

The work behind the title.

My course, The Step Up to Senior Leadership, has twelve lessons arranged into six weeks. It starts with explaining what changed because of your work and finishes with preparing a readiness conversation. Along the way, you practise making recommendations and working across teams. You also learn to delegate to people and to AI.

The aim is to help you develop new leadership skills and replace habits that keep you tied to delivery. The tests help you find a practice focus; the course and cohort provide the structure to work on it.

The larger philosophy.

Earning power × Exit power = Optionality. The first part is developing the skills to take broader leadership responsibility. The second is using the value you create to build more choice over your work and life.

The Optional Career is where I explore both sides. The course develops a practical part of earning power; the writing keeps the wider purpose in view.
