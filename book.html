The idea behind the book

Make your next move
a choice.

You can earn well and still feel unable to leave a job. The salary has grown, but so have the commitments around it. A lower-paying role might suit you better. An idea might deserve a year of your attention. Neither feels possible when the household needs every month’s income.

This book connects the work that will make your time more valuable with the financial decisions that will give some of that time back to you.

You may want to keep climbing. You may want to build something of your own or work at a different pace. The aim is to make those decisions with a clearer view of what your family will need.

Who is this book for?

Indian professionals who want to grow their earning power while building more choice around work and family life. It is especially relevant if a good income has not yet translated into the freedom to change direction.

Is this about retiring early?

It is about having choices throughout your working life. Those choices may include pursuing a bigger role, building something independently or taking time away. Leaving work permanently is one possibility, rather than the goal everyone must pursue.
