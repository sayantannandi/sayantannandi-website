The Optional Career

One path in eight weeks. Get past the middle-management ceiling. Turn your raises into runway. Then decide what the choices are for.

Three ways to startTake the check first

Who it is for

Managers who are good at their job, and who see themselves in some of these.

  • You were told to "be more strategic", and nobody said what to do differently.
  • Your updates are full of work, and your director still cannot repeat them.
  • You were rated "exceeds" again, and the promotion went to someone else.
  • You got a raise, and the EMI grew with it.
  • Last year's bonus went on a holiday and a phone.
  • You disagree in your head and agree in the meeting.

Written especially for managers in technology, product, data and GCCs. If your pay comes with a bonus or RSUs and your updates go to a director in another country, the examples will feel familiar. Managers from other fields are welcome too.

The path, in eight weeks

Five weeks on earning more, two on depending less, and one on what the choice is for. Each week has two lessons, a workbook and one thing to try at work or on your own numbers. Weeks one to four are open now. You can read a free essay from every week before you join.
