Privacy

Your information
and your choices.

The test report request and newsletter subscription are separate choices.

The two free tests

Your answers stay in the current browser tab while you complete the Senior-Level Judgment Check or Leadership Visibility Check. They are sent only when you submit the report form at the end.

The report form submits your first name and email with the test identifier and version. It also includes your selected answers, the practice result and the report text, plus your button choice and the version of the notice you saw. Netlify processes and stores the submission. This lets the result request be associated with the correct report.

Your report is shown on the page after an accepted submission, and you can download it. The email connection shares your name, email and a private report link with MailerLite to fulfil the request. Requests can be held while email delivery is being set up or retried after a temporary failure.

Your private report link

The email link opens your latest attempt at that test. Anyone who has the link can read the report, so share it only if you intend to share the result. The link expires 180 days after the latest report update. This expiry limits access through the link; it does not automatically delete the underlying form submission or purchase records.

The newsletter choice

On a test form, receiving The Optional Career and course or cohort invitations is an optional choice made with the “Show my report and join the letter” button. Choosing “Show my report only” does not sign you up for those emails. On the direct newsletter form, the Subscribe button confirms the subscription described beside it. There is no separate checkbox.

Book preorder announcements

If you request the book announcement, I collect your first name and email through Netlify Forms to contact you about preordering the book. This request does not create an order or subscribe you to The Optional Career. The book announcement list is kept separate from the course and newsletter lists. You can withdraw your request by emailing hello@sayantannandi.com.

Other practice resources

The worked sample and editable leadership brief can be used without an email. Notes entered in the brief stay in the browser tab and are not submitted. The older eight-situation Skills Check also keeps answers in the tab. Reloading clears unsent answers and notes; downloads save a file to your own device.

Service providers

Netlify hosts the site and processes forms. MailerLite is the email provider. I do not sell or rent the mailing list. Report requests and purchase records can be stored as contact profiles without permission to send the newsletter. The newsletter sequence requires your separate subscription choice.

Purchases

TagMango and Razorpay handle the relevant purchases and payment information. I use purchase and contact details to provide access and support. This site does not ask you to enter payment-card information.

Site operation

The test pages do not use advertising pixels or browser storage for your answers. Hosting and email providers may process technical information needed to operate their services. Please keep confidential employer or customer information out of forms.

Contact and unsubscribe

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using their unsubscribe link, or contact hello@sayantannandi.com about your subscription, access or deletion request. Information needed for purchase records or applicable obligations may need to be retained.

Starting Point Check

Your twelve answers stay in the tab until you submit an email report request. You can see and download your report without submitting. The optional request sends your name, email, answer ranges and current decision through Netlify Forms. A private report and selected result fields can be passed to MailerLite for delivery when that email route is enabled. Choosing the newsletter is a separate checkbox. A financial range is still personal information; no exact financial amounts are requested.

Runway calculator

The calculator operates only in your browser. Its reserve, cost and income inputs are not submitted, stored in browser storage, placed in URLs, or sent to analytics. The calculator does not use advertising pixels. Clearing or leaving the page clears those values.

Printable requests and future cohorts

Requesting a chapter or worksheet email copy permits its delivery and three related follow-ups; it does not subscribe you to the weekly newsletter unless you select that option. Next-cohort requests permit date announcements. Requests are saved through Netlify while email delivery is being connected. Immediate printable downloads are available without email.

January cohort intake and recordings

Netlify processes your intake: name, purchase email, payment reference, role, optional company and the situation you choose to submit. These are used to verify payment, provide access and prepare teaching. Google Meet hosts the sessions and recordings, shared with cohort members for 90 days after the final session. Keep confidential details out of submissions and discussions. Do not share other participants’ work outside the group. Contact hello@sayantannandi.com about access or deletion; payment and other records may need to be retained.
