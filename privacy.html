Privacy

What happens to your data

The diagnostics and calculators

Every number you type into a calculator, test or scorecard stays in your browser until you fill in the email form at the end and press the button. If you close the tab before that, it is gone. Nothing is transmitted, stored or logged.

Your email address

If you subscribe or ask for a reading, I collect your email address, your name if you give it, and the answers or scores you chose to send. I use them to send you what you asked for, notes relevant to your result, and the weekly letter. That is all.

Payments

Kits and the cohort are paid through Razorpay, and the self-paced course through TagMango. They handle your card or UPI details under their own terms. I see your name, email and what you bought, which I use to deliver it and to answer questions about it.

What I do not do

I do not sell, rent or share the list. I do not run advertising on this site. There are no tracking pixels from advertising networks.

Leaving

Every email has an unsubscribe link and it works immediately. If you would rather be deleted entirely, write to me and I will remove you and confirm.

Who else touches it

The site is hosted on Netlify and form submissions pass through their infrastructure. Email is sent through MailerLite. Both hold data on my behalf under their own terms.

Not advice

I am not a SEBI-registered investment adviser. Nothing here is investment advice, nothing is personalised to your situation, and the tools are educational. Verify anything that matters with a registered professional.
