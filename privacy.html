Privacy

Your information
and your choices.

The free practice resources can be used without providing an email address.

Skills Check and leadership brief

The Skills Check answers and the text you enter in the leadership brief remain in the current browser tab. The site does not send those answers or notes with signup forms or store them in browser storage. They are cleared when you reload or leave the page. Downloads create a file on your own device.

When you submit a form

The site collects the name, email address, consent choice and any optional goal you submit. Forms are processed and stored through Netlify. Please keep confidential employer, customer and personal information out of the optional goal field.

The course and cohort forms request updates about those offers. The newsletter form requests The Optional Career and occasional course or cohort invitations. Signing up for one form does not automatically select the other lists.

Email and service providers

Netlify hosts this site and processes its forms. MailerLite is used for email delivery. These services process relevant information on my behalf under their own policies. I do not sell or rent the mailing list.

Existing purchases

TagMango and Razorpay process the relevant purchases and payment details. I use purchase and contact information to provide access and support. This website does not ask you to enter payment-card information.

Site operation

The new pages do not load advertising pixels or send Skills Check answers to an analytics service. Hosting and email providers may process technical data needed to operate their services.

Contact and unsubscribe

You can use the unsubscribe link in marketing emails or write to hello@sayantannandi.com about your subscription, access or deletion request. Information needed to maintain purchase records or meet applicable obligations may need to be retained.
