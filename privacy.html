Privacy

What happens to your data

The calculators

Every number you type into the runway calculator, the Exit Power Score or the Earning Power Audit stays in your browser. Nothing is transmitted, stored or logged unless you fill in the email form at the end and press the button. If you close the tab, it is gone.

Your email address

If you subscribe or ask for a worksheet, I collect your email address, your name if you give it, and whatever you typed into the optional boxes. I use it to send you what you asked for and the weekly letter. That is all.

If you sent a diagnostic score along with your email, I use it to send you notes relevant to your result.

What I do not do

I do not sell, rent or share the list. I do not run advertising on this site. There are no tracking pixels from advertising networks.

Leaving

Every email has an unsubscribe link and it works immediately. If you would rather be deleted entirely, write to me and I will remove you and confirm.

Who else touches it

The site is hosted on Netlify and form submissions pass through their infrastructure. Email is sent through an email service provider. Both hold data on my behalf under their own terms.

Not advice

I am not a SEBI-registered investment adviser. Nothing here is investment advice, nothing is personalised to your situation, and the tools are educational. Verify anything that matters with a registered professional.
